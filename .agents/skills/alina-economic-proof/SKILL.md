# Alina Economic Proof

Economic proof is net and executable, not theoretical.

Require frozen hypothesis/config, disjoint held-out OOS/forward evidence, exact costs, fees/rebates/funding, slippage, latency, fill feasibility, capacity, capital-time, closed-position accounting, provenance and no-lookahead.

Report ROI, PnL, PF, drawdown, ES, hit rate, fill/capacity, latency and sample/effective-sample counts as applicable.

Search/tuning feedback that has been observed is exploratory and cannot become final proof without refreezing and new disjoint evidence.

Missing proof-critical costs, rules, timing, or data => UNMEASURABLE/UNCERTAIN, never an optimistic default.

Paper/read-only only; no profitability guarantee.
